# Fixli — Trust, Safety & Compliance

## 1. Professional verification tiers

### Tier 0 — Registered
Basic account verification.

### Tier 1 — Identity verified
Government ID and identity verification where supported.

### Tier 2 — Trade verified
Relevant license/certification verified for a regulated service.

### Tier 3 — Enhanced trust
Additional checks such as insurance or jurisdiction-specific screening.

Every badge must have:
- exact meaning
- verification date
- expiration if applicable
- jurisdiction

## 2. Safety

Customer:
- trusted contact
- provider identity card
- live trip/job status
- emergency access
- report unsafe behavior
- block/restrict communication
- safety center

Professional:
- customer identity signals
- job address access only when needed
- report unsafe site
- cancel for safety
- incident support
- emergency contact

## 3. Emergency policy

Emergency service categories:
- fire
- gas leak
- electrical fire/smoke
- medical emergency
- violent threat
- trapped person
- major water damage
- dangerous structural issue

For life-threatening emergencies:
1. instruct user to contact local emergency services
2. provide localized emergency number when verified
3. provide basic non-dangerous guidance
4. optionally notify trusted contacts
5. do not imply Fixli is emergency dispatch

For property emergencies:
- route to verified urgent professionals if available
- show ETA and service status
- apply emergency service pricing rules transparently

## 4. Payments protection

- authorization before work where appropriate
- customer approval for changes
- payout after completion/hold period according to local rules
- fraud checks
- dispute window
- evidence collection

## 5. Disputes

Categories:
- no-show
- late arrival
- incomplete work
- poor workmanship
- damage
- price dispute
- unauthorized extra work
- payment issue
- safety incident
- fraud

## 6. Privacy

Data minimization:
- collect only what the workflow needs
- exact location only when necessary
- verification documents segregated
- sensitive media access controlled
- deletion/retention policies
- export/delete workflows where legally required

## 7. Global legal framework

Build configurable compliance, not one global rule set.

Potential regimes depending on market:
- GDPR / UK GDPR
- CCPA/CPRA
- LGPD
- local consumer protection
- local payment regulations
- worker/contractor classification
- identity/KYC obligations
- tax/VAT/GST
- local licensing requirements
- emergency calling regulations

Legal counsel must review each launch market.

## 8. Worker classification

Do not describe professionals as employees unless the local legal structure supports it.

Terms, onboarding, pricing control, scheduling control and operational practices must be reviewed market-by-market.

## 9. Child safety

Default platform age should be 18+ for professional and marketplace contracting unless local legal counsel approves another model.
