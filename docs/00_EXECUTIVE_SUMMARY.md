# Fixli — Executive Product Summary

**Recommended working name:** Fixli  
**Tagline:** Get skilled help. Done right.

## What it is
An AI-native, trust-first marketplace for local skilled services.

## Core promise
Tell us what is wrong — by typing, speaking, or showing a photo — and Fixli helps identify the right service, match a qualified professional, coordinate the visit, protect payment, and preserve a verified service record.

## What makes it different
AI is embedded across the entire job lifecycle:
**Understand → Match → Book → Protect → Prove → Remember**

## Launch focus
Start with home repair & maintenance in one city:
Plumbing, electrical, HVAC, appliance repair, handyman, locksmith, cleaning, painting, carpentry and installation.

## Key strategic recommendations
- Do not launch every category globally on day one.
- Do not paywall emergency access.
- Do not let AI make safety, payment or verification decisions autonomously.
- Use PostgreSQL + PostGIS + Redis before adding search infrastructure.
- Use a modular NestJS backend, not microservices initially.
- Make payments, verification, tax, emergency contacts and service requirements country-configurable.
- Build professional offline support because field connectivity is unreliable.
- Make service history/property assets a long-term retention moat.
