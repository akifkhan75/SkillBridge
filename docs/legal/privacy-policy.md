# Fixli Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2027

Welcome to Fixli. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## Information We Collect
- **Identity Data:** Name, phone number.
- **Location Data:** For matching workers and customers.
- **Transaction Data:** Service histories and payments.

## How We Use It
- To connect customers with service professionals.
- To process payments and ensure platform safety.

## Account Deletion
You can delete your account at any time via the Fixli mobile app under Settings > Profile > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your profile data in accordance with local regulations.

## Contact Us
For any privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@fixli.pk.
