# Fixli Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2027

Welcome to Fixli. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy informs you about how we look after your personal data when you use the Fixli app (for customers and professionals) and tells you about your privacy rights.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Identity and Contact Data:** Name, phone number, and optional email address. Professionals also provide business names, government ID, selfies, and trade certificates for verification.
- **Location Data:** We collect precise or approximate location data from your device (when permission is granted) to accurately match customers with nearby professionals, and to enable navigation during an active service booking.
- **Transaction and Service Data:** Details about jobs requested, offers made, bookings completed, and payments processed (we do not store raw credit card numbers; these are handled securely by PCI-compliant payment gateways).
- **Communication Data:** Chat messages, voice notes, and audio transcripts sent between users on the platform to facilitate services, resolve disputes, and ensure safety.
- **Technical and Device Data:** Push notification tokens, device models, operating systems, and IP addresses for app functionality, debugging, and security.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:
- Connect customers with qualified professionals for home maintenance services.
- Verify the identity and qualifications of professionals (using OCR and human review) to ensure trust and safety.
- Process payments, hold funds in escrow, and manage payouts (ledger).
- Power our AI matching engine and provide transcriptions for voice notes.
- Send transactional notifications (booking updates, new messages) and marketing communications (only if you opt in).
- Resolve disputes, enforce our Terms of Service, and prevent fraud.

## 3. Data Sharing

We share your data with:
- **Other Users:** Customers see a professional’s public profile (name, photo, verified badges, rating). Professionals see a customer’s general area initially; the exact address is only revealed *after* a booking is confirmed.
- **Service Providers:** Third-party vendors who provide IT hosting (e.g., AWS, Render), database management, payment processing, AI transcription (e.g., Gemini), and SMS/OTP delivery.
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law or to protect our legal rights.

## 4. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You can delete your account at any time via the Fixli mobile app under **Settings > Profile > Delete Account**. 

Upon deletion, we will anonymize your profile data. However, we may retain certain transactional records (such as completed jobs, payments, and dispute histories) as required by local tax and accounting laws, or to prevent fraudulent users from re-registering.
