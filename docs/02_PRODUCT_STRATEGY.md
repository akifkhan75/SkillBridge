# Fixli — Product Strategy & Competitive Positioning

## Competitive landscape

The current marketplace category already validates several fundamentals:
- Thumbtack lets customers discover profiles, compare prices, chat and hire professionals, with licenses/reviews and a guarantee layer. 
- Taskrabbit combines local task discovery, upfront pricing, vetted Taskers, secure in-app payment and support/protection. 
- Airtasker uses a post-task → offers → selection model and protected payment. 
- Urban Company demonstrates a more curated, standardized service model across multiple cities/countries.
- Newer AI-native services are moving toward natural-language intake, media context and one-match workflows.

## Strategic conclusion

Do not compete by being “another directory of plumbers.”

Compete on the **service intelligence layer**.

### Fixli differentiation

1. **Tell / Show**
   - Text
   - Voice
   - Photo
   - Video

2. **Understand**
   - AI extracts intent and missing information.
   - Risk engine flags unsafe conditions.

3. **Match**
   - Skills
   - Credentials
   - Distance
   - availability
   - reliability
   - price model
   - customer preferences
   - historical job fit

4. **Protect**
   - identity verification
   - transparent price
   - payment authorization
   - change-order approval
   - evidence
   - dispute workflow

5. **Prove**
   - before/after media
   - checklist
   - arrival/completion events
   - customer confirmation
   - service report

6. **Remember**
   - service history
   - property assets
   - warranties
   - recurring maintenance
   - preferred professionals

## Defensible data flywheel

Every completed job can improve:
- category classification
- job duration estimates
- local price bands
- provider reliability scoring
- customer preferences
- repeat-job prediction
- demand forecasting
- service-area supply planning

The flywheel must be privacy-safe and must not expose one customer's private information to another.

## Recommended initial vertical

Start with **home repair & maintenance**, not every possible service.

Launch categories:
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- HVAC/AC
- Appliance repair
- Handyman
- Locksmith
- Cleaning
- Painting
- Carpentry/furniture
- Installation

Expand later into:
- Moving
- Landscaping
- Pest control
- Auto/mobile mechanics
- Property maintenance
- Commercial facilities
- Event services

## Marketplace strategy

Do not launch globally at once.

Use a city-by-city liquidity model:
- 1 launch city
- 5–10 high-frequency categories
- dense professional supply
- strong verification
- controlled service radius
- local operations team
- measured service quality

Then replicate the playbook.

## Trust moat

A provider should accumulate a portable “Fixli Reputation” based on:
- verified identity
- verified credentials
- completed jobs
- on-time rate
- cancellation rate
- customer ratings
- dispute history
- rehire rate
- response time
- category-specific performance

Avoid a simplistic single score. Show explainable badges and category-specific signals.


## Research note

This strategy was informed by current public product materials from Thumbtack, Taskrabbit, Airtasker and Urban Company. These products validate the importance of profiles, reviews, matching/discovery, booking, pricing transparency, protected payment and trust/safety. The goal is not to copy them, but to build a stronger AI-native workflow around those proven marketplace primitives.
